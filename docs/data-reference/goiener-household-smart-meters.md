# GoiEner household smart-meter data

**Fetched 2026-11-17** (by hand with `curl`, not a script) into `data/goiener/raw/` (gitignored): `metadata.csv` (5.6 MB), `imp-post.tzst` (508 MB), `imp-pre.tzst` (792 MB), from Zenodo record 7362094 ("GoiEner smart meters data", Quesada et al., *Scientific Data* 2024, doi 10.1038/s41597-023-02846-0), licence CC BY 4.0. MD5 sums match Zenodo's. Not extracted yet (disk: about 17 GB free; `imp-post` expands to about 4 GB in 17,519 CSV files; `imp-pre` not checked). The lockdown file `imp-in.tzst` (556 MB), the raw 8 GB zip and a newer Version 7.1 record (22313409, September 2026, about 7.6 GB) were not fetched.

- **What it is:** hourly electricity use of 25,559 anonymised supply points of a renewable retail cooperative (GoiEner), end of 2014 → early June 2022; imputed series (`imp-*`); `imp-pre` before 2020-03-01, `imp-in` 2020-03-01 → 2021-05-30, `imp-post` after 2021-05-30.
- **Metadata fields:** start and end dates, missing-sample counts, contract dates, `contracted_tariff`, `self_consumption_type`, contracted power `p1`-`p6`, province, municipality (> 50,000 inhabitants), postal code, CNAE activity.
- **Traps (verified in the metadata):** `contracted_tariff` is mostly the *current* tariff: 22,533 are 2.0TD, 1,146 are 3.0TD, 968 are 2.0A, 529 are 2.0DHA, 188 are missing, the rest 6.1TD/6.2TD and legacy names. Of the 14,686 supply points whose series span before 2020-03-01 and after 2021-09-30, 13,988 are 2.0TD and 637 are 3.0TD. `self_consumption_type` is missing for 23,115. Customers are concentrated in Gipuzkoa (9,291), Bizkaia (6,446), Navarra (3,497) and Araba (2,649); Madrid has 1,110. They are cooperative members on free-market contracts: the retail price they paid is not in the data, so a household's dose (what its price did when the time-of-use tariff changed) has to be assumed from its tariff type.
- **3.0TD households have no usable pre-period (verified 2026-11-17):** 92% of the 577 selected 3.0TD supply points start in January-February 2020 (median 2020-02-07), so they have about three weeks before the lockdown; only 34 have 250 or more pre-lockdown weekdays. The raw archive (`raw.tzst`, 2.0 GB, fetched and then deleted) shows the same start dates, so it holds no hidden history. A dose-response across 2.0TD and 3.0TD customers is not feasible with this dataset. 2.0TD supply points start much earlier (median 2017-11-09).
- **Use:** household-level heterogeneity, and a dose-response across 2.0TD against 3.0TD customers (aggregate dose 0.78 against 0.31, see `docs/specs/2026-11-17-group-comparison-design.md`); the Portugal-difference control carries over by differencing each household's weather-and-calendar error against the Portuguese error.
