# The Spanish time-of-use tariff reform: legal sources (read in the BOE, 2026-11-12)

What the reform is, with the primary text for each step of the charge path. Read from the BOE's own pages (`https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=<BOE id>`). Quoted amounts are €/kWh as printed; €/MWh = ×1000. The BOE's consolidated pages are informative, not legally binding.

## The two components, and where they come from

The ESIOS series 1876 ("Desglose peaje por defecto 2.0TD: Peajes y cargos") is the sum of two regulated parts, set by different bodies:
- **Tolls (peajes de acceso)**, the transport and distribution network charge: CNMC, methodology Circular 3/2020, values by resolution.
- **Charges (cargos del sistema)**, the system charge (renewable support, deficit, non-peninsular costs): Government, methodology Real Decreto 148/2021, values by ministerial order.

Tariff segments in the charges orders are numbered 1–6 and are **not named** in the text. Segment 1 (three energy periods, two power periods) is 2.0TD by shape; segments 2 and 3 (six periods) are 3.0TD and 6.1TD by shape. The mapping is an inference from period counts, **but it is confirmed numerically for segment 1**: toll plus charge reproduces ESIOS 1876 exactly (below).

## What this settles

1. **Start date.** All tariffs, including 3.0TD and 6.x, apply the new tolls from 1 June 2021 (one resolution, one date clause). The earlier open item is closed.
2. **The 16 September cut.** It is a decree-law provision (RDL 17/2021, DA3), from 16 September to 31 December 2021, to the **charges only**; the network tolls stay. That explains why 1876 falls 76% at peak while the charges fell 96%: the toll part (27.4 of 133.1 at peak) did not move.
3. **The paper's 96%.** It is the charges (RDL 23/2021 preamble), not total tolls and charges. The paper's "96% reduction of system charges" and our −76% are consistent.
4. **ESIOS 1876 is tolls plus charges**, to the cent, for summer 2021.

## Still open

- The **1 April 2022 step** in 1876 (peak 100.8 → 74.4) is not in the 2022 charges order (single price for the year). The source is not identified; likely a toll update or another decree-law. Not to be cited as a policy until found.
- The 2022 charges plus the summer-2021 tolls give 100.3 at peak against 100.8 in 1876, so the tolls probably changed slightly on 1 January 2022; the 2022–2026 toll resolutions were not read.
- The calendar of the six periods (Circular 3/2020, art. 7) is not reproduced in the resolution; needed for any 3.0TD/6.xTD design.
- Segment-to-tariff mapping for segments 2 and 3 is by period count only.
