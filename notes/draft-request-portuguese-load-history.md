## English

**Subject:** Request for historical consumption data by voltage level, 2018-2022 (academic research)

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am [a doctoral student / a researcher] at [institution], working on a study of how electricity demand responds to time-of-use pricing in the Iberian market. Portugal's retail tariff structure is stable across the period we study, which makes it a natural comparison group for Spain, but we lack Portuguese consumption data for the years before 2023.

Your open-data portal publishes the dataset **"Consumo total nacional"** (consumption by voltage level: BT, MT, AT, MAT, every 15 minutes) from 1 January 2023, and **"Consumos faturados por município"** (monthly, by voltage level) from November 2020. Could you tell us whether the same series exist for earlier years and, if so, share them?

What would help most, in order of preference:
1. The 15-minute (or hourly, or daily) national consumption by voltage level (BT, MT, AT, MAT), **1 January 2018 to 31 December 2022**, mainland Portugal, in the same format as the 2023 onward dataset.
2. If that is not available: the monthly billed energy by voltage level and by tariff period (ponta, cheias, vazio normal, super vazio), January 2018 to October 2020, in the format of the existing monthly files.
3. If neither exists: a pointer to the body that holds them (REN, ERSE, DGEG) and whether they are available to researchers.

We would use the data only for aggregate statistical analysis and would cite the source in any publication. Please let us know the licence or conditions of use, and whether you need further information about the project.

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
[name]
[position, institution, email]
