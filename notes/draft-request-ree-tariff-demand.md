**Why this is asked (internal, not in the email).** The small-business (3.0TD) response to the June 2021 reform cannot
be identified from E0. Households dilute the signal, and the season-mirror design closed at step zero with a detectable
elasticity of 0.415 (`docs/specs/2026-11-23-small-business-season-mirror-design.md`). ESIOS publishes measured demand
by access tariff for the low-voltage tariffs only from mid-2024: 1209 (2.0TD) and 2532 (3.0TD). 1209 holds a stray
row on 2019-06-01. Indicators 1208 ("sin tarifa de acceso") and 1200 ("sin nivel de tensión", a voltage-level
series) exist but return no data. The tariff series are published from month M+8. With a 3.0 series back to 2014, the small-business test would be about as
sharp as the medium-firm one (detectable elasticity around 0.02). The CNMC version asks for the monthly fallback by
tariff and period, which the CNMC holds in SINCRO, the settlement database behind its indicator bulletin.
