## 4. Ideas for the design (user discussion, 2026-10-02/03)

The user wants bigger treated and control groups, an integrated model founded in
theory and verifiable in the data, surprises rather than anticipated shifts, and is
open to heterogeneous effects.

- **One condition, every firm:** offer − cost = β·n/|RD′| for every firm, unit and
  period; β = 1 Nash, 0 price-taking.
- **Shift-share instruments for n** (Borusyak, Hull & Jaravel 2022): each firm's dose
  = Σ own assets × shock — own-catchment rain, own regional wind and solar, co-owned
  reactor outages by share (Almaraz 1 down: Iberdrola −52.7%, Endesa −36%, Naturgy
  −11.3% of ~1 GW). Identification from the number of independent shocks (regions ×
  days, outages), not of firms. Needs an exposure table firm × region × technology ×
  date; check whether REE's register gives wind/solar units a region. Reactor output
  from PDBF schedules, not `indisp` (gaps after 2023).
- **Rivals' shocks as a second instrument**, moving |RD′| and not own n.
- **Treated vs control units:** gas plants of firms with hydro (5) against gas plants
  of firms without (Cepsa, Engie, RWE, Bizkaia, Bahía de Bizkaia, TotalEnergies, Axpo;
  ~15 units), same day; north-west vs Ebro rain as a second contrast. Fringe units with
  tiny n/|RD′| as a large built-in control group (theory predicts ≈ 0 response).
- **SUTVA:** rain moves the price and rivals' |RD′| (interference); day effects plus
  the measured |RD′| term carry it. Correlated north-west rain is not SUTVA but costs
  power.
- **CATE:** instrumental forest (Athey, Tibshirani & Wager 2019), clustered by week;
  then the best linear projection on 1/|RD′| (slope = β, intercept 0) and sorted group
  effects (Chernozhukov, Demirer, Duflo & Fernández-Val).
- **Buyers:** value − bid = β·q_buy/|RS′|, value = expected intraday price. Cheap test
  on disk: the day-ahead − intraday price gap should take the sign of the big firms'
  combined n (positive when net sellers, Ito & Reguant 2016; negative when net buyers).
- **Surprises:** unplanned reactor trips; forecast errors (outcome − D-1 forecast) →
  intraday offers; interconnector cuts; events (Filomena 2021-01, Maghreb–Europe
  pipeline closure 2021-11, Valencia floods 2024-10, blackout 2025-04-28).
- **Policies as distributions** (changes-in-changes, Athey & Imbens 2006): gas cap
  2022-06-15 → 2023-12-31 (dose per unit = (gas − reference price)/efficiency); IVPEE
  7% generation tax suspension — Spanish units treated, Portuguese controls, same price
  (check dates); windfall levy 2021 (ownership projected, no 2021 listing).
- **Efficiency from offer slopes fails** (28 manufacturer-efficiency gas units: implied
  0.84 vs JRC 0.53, correlation −0.14): keep JRC; use the gas cap's first day as a
  pass-through test instead.
