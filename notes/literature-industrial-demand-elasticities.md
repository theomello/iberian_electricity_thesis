# Industrial electricity demand elasticities: what the literature reports, by margin

Written 2026-11-17 for the proto-meta-analysis. Values are as stated in the papers in `literature/` (markdown in `literature/md/`); rows marked † are carried from `docs/business-demand-evidence.md` and were not re-read in this pass. **Margin matters more than the number**: hourly or within-day responses, month-scale and annual responses are different objects, and none of these is directly comparable with a same-hour elasticity from a wind-driven price move.

## Where we sit
- **Against exposed-customer, within-day estimates (0 to -0.27, substitution 0.10-0.27):** our exposed-share translation (aggregate bound -0.021, exposed share 0.32-0.50) bounds the exposed customers' same-hour response at about -0.04 to -0.07, below the shoulder values of Patrick and Wolak and the Boisvert range, which are for customers who face and manage hourly prices and are not elasticities of load to a price level.
- **Against the sustained-level estimates (-0.1 to -0.6):** not comparable and not tested here (see the amendment in the day-level spec).
- **Against Spanish evidence:** the only Spanish industrial elasticities found are Labandeira et al.'s near-zero values (-0.015 to -0.024) from average prices; Moral-Carcedo and Pérez-García report that Spanish industrial consumers' production cuts to save electricity cost do not seem profitable (citing Torriti, Hassan and Leach 2010).

A survey with no estimate, but it is the source of three Spanish facts: (1) about 200 industries above 5 MW were on voluntary interruptible special tariffs from 1988, with requests of 45 minutes to 12 hours and, per ETSO, 0-4 days of requests a year (10 in 2001); (2) a study commissioned under Royal Decree 2392/2004 on 87 large time-of-use customers found that cutting production to save on the bill was not profitable; (3) manageable power as a share of contracted power ranges from 22% (car industry) to 96% (fish farms, airports). The first two are second-hand citations in the paper.
